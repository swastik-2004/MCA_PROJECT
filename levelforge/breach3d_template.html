HOW TO PLAY

Goal: reach the glowing extraction portal. Some levels need the magenta keycard to open the blast door first.

Hunter drones hunt you and fire plasma bolts; you can sidestep them. Proximity mines on the floor blink red: shoot them from a distance to detonate them safely. Med-kits restore integrity; data cores are optional score.

Controls: mouse look · WASD move · click fire · SHIFT sprint · SPACE dash (brief invulnerability) · R reload · arrow keys also turn · ESC pause · M music · G graphics quality (switches automatically if the game runs slowly).

The game uses the same values as the AI's digital twin: the same starting health, drone health, mine damage and med-kit healing. The twin is a simplified turn-based model of this game.
