The words to explain
Digital twin

A virtual copy of a real thing that stays linked to it: data flows from the real thing into the copy, and what we learn in the copy flows back.

Physical twin

The real thing being copied. Here: the playable 3D game and the people who play it.

Fidelity

How closely the twin matches reality. Ours copies layout and numbers exactly, but turns real-time skill into probabilities.

What-if analysis

Testing a change in the twin before making it for real: "if we remove this drone, how many players finish?"

Common random numbers

Every candidate level is played with the same dice rolls, so a better score comes from a better level, not luck.

Validation

Checking the twin against real players. Our team found level 3 hardest while the bots rated it slightly easier, so this is our next step.
